About

A warm welcome in Cork

Belvedere Lodge has gained a wonderful reputation for its warm welcome since Tim and Marie McGrath first opened their doors.

We are close to Silver Springs Moran Hotel and convenient to Little Island and Fota. We are ideally placed for touring, directly situated between the Wild Atlantic Way and Ireland's Ancient East touring drives.

Car hire is available at Cork Airport or in Cork city centre.
