Our services

Cajun cafe favorites, specialty cocktails and live bands, all under one roof.

Cajun Cafe Menu

Our menu features some of the best Cajun food this side of the Mississippi, from gumbo to fried crawfish tails.

Award Winning Burgers

Try our award winning Meatloaf Cheeseburger or the Pot Roast Burrito.

Po'Boys & Po'Boy Bowls

A traditional Louisiana sandwich on locally baked French bread, with your choice of meat or seafood. Prefer no bread? Order it as a Po'Boy Bowl.

Gumbo & Green Chili

Chicken & Andouille Gumbo and house made pork green chili, available by the cup, bowl or quart.

Cocktails, Beer & Wine

About us

About Our Venue

We are a locally owned Blues Bar and Cajun Cafe, serving live blues music and award winning comfort food in the Wash Park area for over 19 years.

We host great local acts and amazing national acts. Alongside the blues, we showcase Classic Rock, Swamp and Outlaw Country bands.

Every year we host a Fat Tuesday party with a crawfish boil and a Cajun band.
