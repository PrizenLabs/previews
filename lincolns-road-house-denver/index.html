About

About Our Venue

We are a locally owned Blues Bar and Cajun Cafe, serving live blues music and award winning comfort food in the Wash Park area for over 19 years.

We host great local acts and amazing national acts. Alongside the blues, we showcase Classic Rock, Swamp and Outlaw Country bands.

Every year we host a Fat Tuesday party with a crawfish boil and a Cajun band.
