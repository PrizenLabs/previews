About

About The Vat House

The Vat House Bar is a traditional Irish pub, named after the vat house in the Guinness Brewery at St. James Gate, Dublin. In the final stages of brewing, Guinness is stored in large copper vats and left to mature, allowing the flavour to develop.

The interior reflects that maturing and developing process. The timber floors are pitch pine, mahogany and oak, salvaged from the original vat houses in the St. James Gate Brewery. Elegant brickwork and cobble floors mirror the streets of Temple Bar itself.

The Vat House prides itself on offering unrivalled levels of quality and comfort to all our visitors.
