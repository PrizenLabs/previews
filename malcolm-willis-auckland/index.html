What we do

Our coating and finishing services

Chemical and wet spray coating for metal finishing and protection against corrosion.

Black Oxide

A decorative finish for ferrous metals where no dimensional change is required. Suits printing press parts, conveyors, tools, lathe parts, fine springs and delicate instruments, with rust inhibiting properties.

Ferrophos Black Satin Finish

A black satin finish that adds anti-corrosion and heat resistance, with good abrasive resistance. Parts can be coated when assembled and will not lock up as with powder coat.

Spray Painting

We can spray paint practically any items you bring in, such as springs, screw heads, bolt heads and pins, to match whatever background. Paint can be client provided or we can provide it.

Molybond Dry Lubricant Coating

A dry lubricant bonded onto the surface of steel, brass, aluminum and stainless steel. On stainless steel bolts and nuts it stops galling, which saves cutting bolts during disassembly.

Protective & Anti-Corrosive Coatings

Coatings that protect components in adverse conditions. Molybond-coated components can withstand 500+ hours of salt spray.
