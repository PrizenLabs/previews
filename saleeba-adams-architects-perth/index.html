About the studio

Saleeba Adams Architects was founded in Western Australia in 1979 on a fundamental commitment to design excellence and quality service, and a clear understanding of the contribution this makes to user satisfaction and commercial success.

That commitment has built a reputation for superior service and recognition through numerous design awards, including the prestigious AIA 'George Temple Poole Award'.

Under Directors Trevor Saleeba and Grant Adams, the firm has worked throughout Western Australia and in Indonesia and Brunei.
