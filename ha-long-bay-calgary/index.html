About

Our Story

Ha Long Bay Vietnamese Cuisine is family owned and operated since 1999. Our family has a long background in the restaurant industry with several other establishments throughout Calgary, Alberta.

When you walk in you will be greeted with a cheerful welcome and the aroma of BBQ. We believe in freshness, wholesome, and homemade food, and only the finest ingredients go into our dishes.
