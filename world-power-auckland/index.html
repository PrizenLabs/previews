What we do

What we do

Marine, power generation and industrial and commercial electrical work, all from one team.

Marine Electrical

Marine electrical is a specialised field. World Power covers all aspects for yachts, launches, commercial vessels and superyachts.

Generators & Control Systems

As Woodward authorised system integrators, we are involved in the design, installation and commissioning of Woodward control equipment, automatic voltage regulators and speed control equipment.

Building WOF 12A Certification

If your building lists SS4 Emergency lighting and SS14.1 Emergency Power systems on its Building Warrant of Fitness, we can provide the testing and certification to allow it to gain its next Bwof.

Commercial & Industrial Electrical

Installation, machinery repair and modifications, upgrades, and PLC fault finding and installation, backed by experience in building and machinery maintenance.

Telecommunication Site Services

Critical site maintenance for the telecommunication industry, including generator testing, battery discharge tests and condition reporting, alarm testing and routine maintenance.

Industrial Electronics Repair

Circuit board repairs down to component level for older or obsolete equipment. Where repair isn't economic, the team can retro fit or source replacements.

About us

About World Power

World Power Limited brings experience and expertise to marine electrical work, covering everything from yachts and launches to commercial vessels and superyachts.

We are experts in generators and generator control systems, and an Authorised System Integrator (ASI) for Woodward covering New Zealand and the South pacific.

Our industrial and commercial division handles building maintenance, telecommunication and industrial electronics, from designing control equipment for special projects through to installation and build.
