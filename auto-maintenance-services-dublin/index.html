What we do

Our Services

From routine servicing to diagnostics, tyres and repairs, everything your vehicle needs is handled in our workshops.

Fiat & Alfa Romeo Servicing

We specialise in Fiat and Alfa Romeo vehicles, and we process warranty claims for them. Servicing at the recommended intervals is vital.

Engine & Electrical Diagnostics

Mechanical and electrical engine diagnostics, plus the Brain Bee Scan Tool System for systems such as engine management, air bag/SRS systems and ABS on all vehicles.

Tyre Service

Replacement tyres, tracking and balancing.

Transmission & Panel Work

In-house transmission repair service and panel shop facilities.

Pre N.C.T & Emission Testing

Pre N.C.T testing, emission testing for petrol and diesel vehicles, and head light alignment.

Collection, Return & Valet
