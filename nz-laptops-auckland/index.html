About us

About NZLaptops

We source product locally as well as overseas. Every laptop has its hard drive permanently wiped of any data, then receives a total clean install with the correct drivers, service packs, updates and anti virus protection, so you receive a computer that is virus free and working perfectly.

Before dispatch, computers are cleaned and packaged professionally in our own custom made boxes to minimise the chance of damage during transit.

Because our technicians work on laptops day in day out, they offer that knowledge as after sales support. We want our clients fully satisfied with a problem free purchase.
