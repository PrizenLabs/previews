About

A Georgian house with a personal touch

Lough Mahon House is a family run, comfortable Georgian house where the personal attention of Tony & Margot makes it a great place to stay.

You'll find us on the N8 Dublin / Waterford Road, next to the Clayton Hotel Silver Springs, just 5 minutes by car from Cork City centre and 2 minutes from the Lee Tunnel.
