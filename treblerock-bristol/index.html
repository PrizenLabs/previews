New, Used & Vintage Guitars

Browse a fantastic range of new and used guitars, along with a selection of vintage electric and acoustic guitars. Acoustic, electric, bass, ukulele and left handed guitars are all part of the range.

Amplifiers & Effects

Guitar amplifiers, bass amplifiers and effects pedals. We will even help match amplifiers and effects to your new guitar or existing guitar.

Guitar Repairs & Setups

From a basic restring through to a full fret levelling and reprofile, we can offer this service from within the store. Headstock repairs and damaged necks and bodies are also within our remit.

Amp & Effects Repairs

A full repair service for amplifiers, FX pedals and speaker cabinets. We aim to turn around repairs within one week, parts permitting, and an express repair service is also available.

Lessons & Tuition

Steve Treble offers personal tuition in the Bristol shop for guitar and ukulele, and even tuition in guitar and amplifier setup.

We Buy Used Guitars

Got a used guitar to sell? Call us or drop in for more details.

About

Guitar advice from people who listen

Here at Treblerock we do not just sell guitars and amplifiers. We listen to you, talk over your requirements and work with you to provide a guitar, amplifier or both that is exactly what you want.

The team is headed up by Steve Treble, who has been playing guitar since he was a child and has been running Treblerock since 1991. Steve has an expert knowledge of all things musical and can help you choose the setup you need to suit your style and requirements.

Our service extends to aftercare of instruments and equipment, servicing and repair along with tuition and consultancy.
