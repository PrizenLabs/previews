About us

About Miss Molly's

Miss Molly's is an east coast Italian-inspired bakery offering a tasty array of unique items, including specialty cakes and desserts, calzones, rotating savory specials, and other items.

You'll find us at 2600 East Colfax Avenue, on the corner of Colfax and Elizabeth in Congress Park in Denver, across the street from the Tattered Cover bookstore and East High School.
