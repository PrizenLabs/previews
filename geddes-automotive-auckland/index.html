What we do

Repairs and servicing for cars, vans and trucks

We have the mechanics, technicians and equipment to maintain and repair your vehicle's mechanical and auto electrical systems.

Auto electrical repairs

We diagnose and fix dash fault lights, wiring faults and computer faults, and test and replace alternators and batteries.

Fault scanning & diagnosis

We use a number of different scanners to diagnose engine and electrical faults across a very wide range of vehicles, models and years.

Brake repairs

Discs machined or replaced and brake pads fitted.

Cambelts & timing chains

Cambelt kits and water pumps replaced, and timing chain faults found and repaired.

Clutch, CV joints & bearings

Clutches, pressure plates and flywheels replaced, along with CV joints and boots and front and rear wheel bearings.

Mufflers & exhausts

New and some second hand mufflers and exhausts for cars, vans, off-road vehicles and trucks, in petrol and diesel.

WoF & servicing

We provide a WoF service and vehicle servicing for cars, vans and trucks.

Breakdown towing & recovery

If your vehicle has broken down, we offer a full tow and recovery service and can repair it too.

About us

About Geddes Automotive

Geddes Automotive is an MTA garage in Onehunga, qualified in all mechanical and auto electrical repairs. The team has repaired and modified over 450,000 vehicles for satisfied customers.

Peter Geddes set many Auckland and nine national New Zealand drag speed records in vehicles customised by Peter and his team. Unitec New Zealand invited Peter onto their Vehicle Technology Advisory Committee, and voted for him as Chairman for a number of years running.

We repair all makes, models and years of vehicle, and we give you cost options so you choose the level and quality of repair you can afford.
