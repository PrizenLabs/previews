About us

A great base for exploring Bristol

Clift Guest House is ideally located for exploring Bristol or enjoying the many festivals, sporting events and entertainments that occur in the city.

North Street, approximately three minutes walk away, hosts the Tobacco Factory and lots of other cafés, pubs and restaurants. Ashton Gate Football Stadium is approximately 5 minutes walk from the guest house.

Guests can use the guest lounge and 2 fully equipped kitchens, with breakfast provided on a help yourself basis.
