About us

A family-run business at the heart of the community

Harris Heating was established by Trevor Harris and is now owned and run by his daughter and son in law, Becci & Lee Tate, with the same core values customers have grown to love.

Our team of highly qualified Gas Safe plumbing and heating engineers brings more than 20 years' experience to every job. We pride ourselves on outstanding customer service, quality workmanship, straight-talking, open and honest advice, and fair and transparent pricing.

Our aim is simple: to help customers keep their properties warm and safe across Leeds and West Yorkshire.
