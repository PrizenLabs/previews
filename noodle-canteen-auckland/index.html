What we do

From the wok to your box

Choose from our wide variety of dishes, order online, or build your own noodle box.

Wok Tossed Noodles

Dishes like Vegetarian Soy Noodles: egg noodles mixed with tofu, mushrooms and fresh vegetables in a dark soy sauce.

Soup Based Noodles

Try the Combination Soup: egg noodles with prawns, beef, pork and chicken served with fresh vegetables in a chicken soup.

Create Your Own Noodle Box

If the menu isn't to your taste, pick and choose from a wide range of fresh ingredients to make your own unique noodle box.

Online Ordering

Order online from the Henderson, Royal Oak, City Branch, Te Aro Wellington and Albany stores.

Group Catering

Custom made menus including finger foods for lunch and dinner, for office or social events. Email us your details and we'll be in touch.

Eat In or Take Away

Enjoy your meal straight away at our allocated seating, or take it away to work, home or to share with friends.

About us

Every meal is an experience

Walk in and you'll be greeted by the aroma of delicate spices, sizzling fresh vegetables and the excitement of fast paced traditional Asian wok cooking. Then sit back and enjoy the action from our comfortable bar stools.

Our food is on display at all times and we prepare your meal right in front of you, so you can see the fresh, high quality ingredients for yourself.

Our central vision is to provide fresh, tasty, nutritious and affordable meals to all.
