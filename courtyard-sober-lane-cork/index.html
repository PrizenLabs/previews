The Courtyard

The centrepiece of the venue: spacious, open-air and designed for social energy. Enjoy everything from relaxed daytime drinks to packed nights and major live sporting events.

The Quarter & Lower Deck

A semi-open, social space where relaxed daytime drinks roll into lively evenings. Ideal for guests who want atmosphere without the intensity of a packed dancefloor.

The Southgate Bar

A classic Cork pub experience, full of character and warmth. A more intimate, timeless contrast to the open energy of The Courtyard.

Food Menu

Customer favourites include The Southgate Rib Eye Burger and Stone-Baked Italian Pizzas, plus a new lunch menu with Braised Lamb Shank. Ingredients are sourced from local suppliers wherever possible.

Cocktails & Whiskey

An extensive drinks selection, from classics like Whiskey Sours, Old Fashioneds and Pornstar Martinis to creative cocktails made with bold, flavoursome ingredients.

Live Music, Quiz & Sport

Live music every Friday night, our popular Quiz Night and daily rolling sports coverage across multiple screens.

Events & Parties

Three distinct spaces combined into one seamless venue, so your event can be tailored to the size, style and atmosphere you want, from an intimate gathering to a full-scale celebration.

About

A new chapter in the heart of Cork City

Formerly a school and now proudly under new management and ownership, The Courtyard on Sober Lane is stepping into an exciting new chapter in the heart of Cork City, with fresh ideas, vibrant events and fantastic plans ahead.

Each space has its own atmosphere, and together they create a unique social experience for every occasion. Our covered canopy means the outdoor Courtyard can be enjoyed 365 days a year, whatever the weather. Small and large groups are welcome in our indoor seating areas or the partially covered outdoor courtyard.

With a passionate new team, we look forward to welcoming familiar faces and new guests for great food, drinks and unforgettable moments.
