What we do

What we do

From buying your next bike to keeping it serviced and secure, we have you covered.

  1. 01

    Motorbikes for Sale

    New & second hand motorbikes from sports bikes to cruisers to mopeds. We are always happy to provide advice on the appropriate motorcycle for you.

  2. 02

    Trade-ins

    We are happy to take trade-ins on all our motorbikes for sale, and we will give you a competitive quote fast.

  3. 03

    Motorbike Servicing

    In our fully kitted out workshop we can identify and diagnose any problem with your motorbike. We will only carry out the work that you request.

  4. 04

    Alarms & Electronics

    We are experts in motorbike electronics and motorbike alarm systems, as a Meta Alarms certified dealer & fitter.

  5. 05

    Chains & Disc Locks

    We sell motorbike chains and motorbike disc locks too, which are also a good idea to keep your motorcycle safe.

  6. 06

    Tyres

    We stock tyres from Pirelli, Dunlop, Bridgestone, Michelin and Avon.

About Scott's Bikes

Scottsbikes has been selling and servicing motorbikes in Ireland since 2001. With thousands of satisfied customers, we pride ourselves on our quality of work and our customer service.

When servicing your motorcycle, we give friendly advice on appropriate repairs only if there is a safety issue, and we will only carry out the work that you request.
