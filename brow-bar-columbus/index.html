About Brow Bar

At Brow Bar we shape brows and maintain skin for the perfect, natural beauty palette. Our Artists reveal the beauty of your brows and the natural glow of your skin.

We use the ancient Indian technique of eyebrow threading, following the unique contours of your face. We carry Flormar the professional makeup line, so leave the work to us.
