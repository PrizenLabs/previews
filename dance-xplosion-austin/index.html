What we do

Classes for Every Dancer

From preschool creative dance to pointe and hip hop, Dance Xplosion offers all forms of dance.

Ballet, Pointe & Lyrical

Classes in Ballet, Pointe, Lyrical and Contemporary for dancers ages 2-18.

Jazz, Tap & Hip Hop

Jazz, Tap, Hip Hop, and Leaps & Turns classes, plus School Dance/Drill Team Prep.

Musical Theatre & Acro

Musical Theatre and Acro classes, plus Acro workshops for ages 6-18 in two levels.

Preschool & Toddler Classes

Preschool Creative Dance, Toddler & Hip Bop, and Superhero Tumble for our youngest dancers.

Family Music Classes

Music classes for kids ages 8 months - 4.5 years, with a parent or guardian who actively participates.

Camps & Workshops

Summer classes, camps, intensives and workshops, plus Parents' Day Out with dancing, games, and arts & crafts for ages 4-10.

Master Classes

Guest teachers from around the country hold master classes open to current and future students.

Bright Lights Dancewear

A full retail store in the studio lobby with shoes, tights, clothes, gifts and accessories in youth and adult sizes.

About us

About Dance Xplosion

Dance Xplosion was opened by lifetime dancer Amber Frishman in 2008. Year-round classes are offered, with the regular schedule held from the end of August through May, and an Annual Recital in the spring.

Our goal is not to force training and technique, but to teach, nurture and inspire students as they explore dance and music. Our instructors are highly trained and always striving to learn the latest trends and techniques in their genre of dance.

Our facility has more than 3,300sq feet of space with 3 studio rooms. Each has professional-grade, fully-sprung Marley floors, wall-to-wall mirrors, water fountains, ballet barres and full sound systems.
