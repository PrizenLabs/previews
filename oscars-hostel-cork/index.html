About Oscars Hostel

Oscars Hostel is the latest and most stylish contemporary hostel in Cork city. Formerly a B&B, it provides only the best accommodation and customer service for your money.

Situated close to both train and bus stations, Oscars is ideal for anyone travelling to the peoples' republic. We offer superior service with a personal touch, whether you're visiting Cork for a concert, backpacking or sight-seeing.

Between us, we have years of experience and can suit your taste when it comes to pubs, restaurants, venues, museums, galleries and more.
