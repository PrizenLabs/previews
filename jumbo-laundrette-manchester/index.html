About

A community laundrette in Gorton

Jumbo Laundrette is a family run, community laundrette at 119 Levenshulme Road, Gorton M18 7NF, where customer service and satisfaction come first.

We offer a complete laundry service for domestic and small business customers, and business customers are welcome.

Every possible care will be taken when washing the garment. However, it is the client's responsibility to separate the garment and to highlight the specific cleaning instructions for its care.
