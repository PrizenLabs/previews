RC Models & Sales

Jets, airplanes, helicopters, cars, boats, engines, simulators, nitro fuel, accessories and upgrade parts. Our trained Sales Executives help you find what suits your needs.

Workshop Repairs & Assembly

Our friendly R/C Technicians repair and assemble cars, planes, helicopters, boats and jets. Models we sell are properly set up and tuned before delivery.

First-Flight Training

Beginners get first-flight training on airplanes and helicopters bought from us, led by qualified pilots on a flying field or in a safe designated area.

Custom RC Painting

Our painting section is specialised for RC products, from simple to highly complicated jobs. We have painted canopies, scale bodies, car bodies and boats, plus helmets and bikes.

Spare Parts

We usually have most of the spare parts for the products we sell, with a big store of new and old parts. Send us an inquiry with the part number and we will try our best to arrange it.

Discount Section

A dedicated area of selected, re-priced items that hobbyists may find useful, and a good fit for beginners who wish to spend less.

About

A hobby shop that cares

Hobby Centre was established on the 25th of October 1998. We began with a staff of 4 and a 5,000 sq. ft. showroom, storage and workshop space, and have grown into a name known in RC model sales throughout the Middle East.

We are both distributor and retailer for a number of the world's RC brands, including Kyosho, Tamiya, HPI and DJI. Our drone and technology solutions support industries such as agriculture, construction, surveying, logistics and marine operations.

Our vision is to be the leader resource and service provider for all hobbyists in our region and beyond.
