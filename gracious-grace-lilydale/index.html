What we do

Food, Drinks, Cakes & Events

From casual coffees to hearty breakfasts, chic lunches and indulgent cakes, there is something to entice your taste buds.

All-Day Breakfasts

Hearty breakfasts served until 3pm, from eggs on high top sourdough to the Vegan big breakfast and Mark's big breakfast.

Lunch

Lunch from 12pm, including the Stella burger, Reuben sandwich, risotto and a buffalo chicken salad.

Coffee & Hot Drinks

Cappuccino, latte, macchiato, mocha and more, with alternate milks available, plus hot chocolate made with real Belgium chocolate.

Cakes to Order

Cakes made especially for you or your special occasion, in a range of styles and flavours. Gluten-free options are available. Call or email to enquire about pricing.

Functions & Catering

Birthday parties, social gatherings and corporate functions. We're fully licensed, and your event can flow onto the large private deck.

Wine, Beer & Cider

Sparkling, white, pink and red wines, plus beers and Gracious Grace ciders in pear, apple, peach and strawberry.

About us

Welcome to Gracious Grace Cafe

Our food is fresh, tasty and made to order, using fresh, local Yarra Valley produce. Eat and drink in style overlooking the picturesque, historic Melba Park.

Whether it's a casual coffee, an indulgent cake, a hearty all-day breakfast or a chic lunch, Gracious Grace will entice your taste buds.

We're in Castella Street, right next to the Yarra Ranges Regional Museum.
