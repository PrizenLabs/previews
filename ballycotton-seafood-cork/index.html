What we do

Our Fish & Seafood Range

From fresh catch to smoked fish and artisan oven ready seafood, everything comes from one family owned business.

Fresh Fish

A huge range of fresh fish, brought in by our day boats from the fishing grounds on the south coast. We select the best of the daily catch for our customers.

Shellfish

Fresh and frozen shellfish, processed at our fully licensed fish plant on the shores of Ballycotton Bay.

Traditional Smoked Fish

We smoke our own fish in our own smokehouse using traditional oak methods. Techniques passed from one generation to the next ensure premium smoked fish.

Artisan Oven Ready Seafood

Chowders, seafood pies, sauces and breaded seafood made in our very large commercial kitchen using traditional cooking techniques and natural ingredients sourced locally.

Food Service & Export

We supply the food service sector in Ireland locally and nationally, and export to France, Spain and UK. Wholesale and food service customers are serviced daily using our refrigerated vans and trucks.

About us

Our Story

Established in 1985, Ballycotton Seafood is a family owned and run fish and seafood business. We have our own boats, plus a number of boats that land their catch exclusively to us, so we can select the best of the daily catch for our customers.

We are not just fishmongers. Ballycotton Seafood is a one stop shop for your fish and seafood requirements, with our own smokehouse and a very large commercial kitchen where we cook and bake on a daily basis for our shops.

Today we employ over 40 people in our fish plant and shops. We have 4 Fish Shops, located in the Old English Market Cork, Midleton, Glanmire and Garryvoe.
