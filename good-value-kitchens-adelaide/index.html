About us

About Good Value Kitchens

Good Value Kitchens was founded in 1995 by Lulu Wu. We have been manufacturing stylish and traditional cabinets and cupboards for decades, and have become well-known for superb design and techniques.

We work with a number of local building companies and also accept orders from individual customers. If you want a one-of-a-kind kitchen, everything here is flexible.

An internal tracking system is used to ensure any question or complaint is recorded and handled in time. The standard warranty is five years.
