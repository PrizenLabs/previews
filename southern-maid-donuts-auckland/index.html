About

A Legacy of Great Donuts

Southern Maid has a great history of baking and selling delicious donuts, thanks to the secret recipe of their donut flour. It began in 1937 when J.B. Hargrove started Southern Maid with his wife, Rosalea, who perfected that famous recipe.

The donut flour recipe is the same as it was back in 1937, and the company is still a family operation.

With humble pride, we welcome you to our New Zealand stores.
