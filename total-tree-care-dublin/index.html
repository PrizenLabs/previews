What we do

Our Tree & Hedging Services

A comprehensive service specialising in felling, stump removal, cutting, maintenance, clearances and hedge cutting.

  1. 01

    Tree Felling

    Felling is usually the last resort, but removing a dead or diseased tree makes the area around it a lot safer and allows other trees to thrive. We have felled trees for private individuals, commercial projects, estate maintenance and public projects.

  2. 02

    Stump Removal

    Specialised stump grinding machinery removes unsightly or dangerous stumps safely, even in awkward areas or narrow access. Once complete, you are free to grow grass or pave as desired.

  3. 03

    Tree Maintenance

    Regular maintenance supports the wellbeing of your trees and avoids unnecessary problems in future. We help you identify what your trees need, from crown thinning to deadwood removal.

  4. 04

    Crown Reduction & Raising

    Crown reduction brings an overgrown crown in proportion to the overall tree size. Crown raising removes lower branches to increase the height of the main crown from ground level.

  5. 05

    Tree Overhaul & Deadwood

    A thorough overhaul removes crossing branches, deadwood, diseased wood and broken branches, leaving a healthy tree that continues to grow safely. Deadwood is surveyed and removed safely.

  6. 06

    Site Clearance

    Total clearances of trees, shrubbery and hedges for property development, overgrown gardens and public areas. On completion the site is totally cleared and any tree waste is taken away.

  7. 07

    Hedge Cutting

    From small garden hedges to large boundary hedges, whatever the height. We assess your hedge, recommend the right solution and remove the debris for you.
