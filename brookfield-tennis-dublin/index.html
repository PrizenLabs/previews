About

About Brookfield

Brookfield Lawn Tennis Club is based at Palmerston Park, Dublin 6, with tennis for seniors and juniors and a club calendar of events.

Our new padel venue brings a vibrant, welcoming community for players of all ages and skill levels. It includes on-site changing rooms, racket hire, and access to the club's bar facilities and events.

The club is run by a volunteer committee and a Club Manager, with coaches and Child Protection Officers supporting members.
