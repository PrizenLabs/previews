About

Our story

The doors of Eden Home and Garden opened in 2007 in the heart of Blackrock village.

The inspiration behind Eden was a large showroom housing a collection of furniture and accessories for the home, with a view to creating an eclectic and unique style. Everything is carefully sourced and brought together to create a fresh ambiance.

The collection is sourced all over Europe and further afield, with a lively and eclectic approach to sourcing.
